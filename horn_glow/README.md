# Horn Glow

- **Version:** 1.1.0
- **Author:** modChad

A unicorn's horn magic now glows around the horn itself, instead of as a round disc of light floating in front of it.

- The glow wraps the whole horn in the pony's magic colour, see-through in the middle and brightest at its edges, with soft ripples and a rounded, gently flickering top.
- Celestia and Luna glow along their own long princess horns.
- A gentle hum rises and fades with the glow, in place of the loud cast sparkle sound. The sparkles stay.
- Things lifted with magic keep the game's usual aura.

## Works with other mods (1.1.0)

Kissing's build-up and Idle Life's magic sparks use the horn glow when this mod is on.

## Install

Put `horn-glow-v1.1.0.pck` in the game's `mods` folder and switch it on in **Settings → Mods**.
