# Better Balloon

- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **By:** modChad
- **Made for:** Home: Equestria 0.3.1-maredev
- **Works on:** desktop (VR keeps the game's own Helm grip; not tested)

The hot-air balloon, made for ponies: proper flying controls, a basket ponies can see out of, passengers who look over the side and talk together, and easier boarding. Replaces the **Balloon Boarding** mod: switch that one off (if both are on, Better Balloon leaves boarding to it).

## Flying

- **At the Helm** (grab it with E or LMB, as before):
  - **W / S**: forward and back (the gate side is the front)
  - **A / D**: sideways
  - **Q / E**: turn the balloon, gently
  - **Space / Ctrl**: rise and descend
  - **Mouse**: look around freely
  - **LMB or Esc**: let go
- **Idle drift.** Let go of the Helm in the air and the balloon drifts slowly on a wandering breeze, turning a little now and then. The Lift and Vent ropes still work as normal. No drift once landed or with the gate open.
- Ponies aboard turn with the basket, and so do you. Your briefcase, a pony's card, the clothes menu and the Tab menu ride along with you.

## The basket

- **Built for ponies.** The rim is 0.80 m above the deck instead of 1.2 m, so even a Canon-size pony can see over it. The balloon's ropes are rebuilt to reach it, tied round the corner posts.
- **Lean over the side.** Stand at a wall facing out and hold **Ctrl** to lean out over the rim and look down. Let go to stand back up.

## Passengers

- **Looking out.** While you fly, every so often a passenger gets up, goes to a free spot by the wall, rears up with her forelegs over the rim to look out for a while, then goes back to her seat. Press **6** aboard to have the nearest one do it now (or stop).
- **Talking together.** Talk to one passenger and the others join in, as a group conversation.
- **Carry a pony in.** Stand in the balloon and set a pony down in the basket: she stays aboard as a passenger instead of hopping straight back out.
- **Bring more than one.** Passengers wait while you step out to fetch another pony (up to 2 minutes, within 15 m). The basket holds you and three Canon-size ponies.
- **Followers who stop short.** A pony following you who gets stuck before the ramp (bigger ponies like Princess Celestia) still climbs in after a couple of seconds. You need to be aboard, landed, with the gate open; ponies still can't wander in on their own.

## Install

Put `better-balloon-v1.0.0.pck` in the game's `mods` folder and switch it on in **Settings → Mods**. Remove `balloon-boarding-v1.0.0.pck` if you have it.

## Things to know

- Typing in the chat lets go of the Helm (as in the game); grab it again after sending.
- Turning doesn't check for trees or buildings; give the basket some room.
- A pony helped in from far away hops the whole way in one go.
- Known: a passenger's tail can sit between her legs when she rears up (the game's tail bones); petting a passenger in flight can look jerky.
- The log notes what the mod does (lines starting `[better_balloon]`).
