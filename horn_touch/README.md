# Horn Touch (modChad)

Grab or stroke a unicorn's horn.

- **Desktop:** hold the left mouse button on her horn (the normal pet) and move the mouse along it.
- **VR (attempted, untested):** grip her horn and slide your hand along it.

**Below Close Friend** (or while she's angry at you), she blasts you away with her magic: knocked
back and dazed (blurred, doubled, swaying sight, no movement) for 15 s, or 30 s for an
alicorn. Do it again within 30 s and she bucks you. Each unwanted touch costs a little trust.

**Close Friend and up**, she lets you, shy and flustered. Keep stroking and her horn sparkles more
and more until it overloads in a burst of sparkles in her magic colour. She needs 20 s after that.

**Word gets around:** she tells you off, and the nearest onlooker (two once you make a habit of
it) reacts in their own way. Ponies within 10 m (and she herself) remember what happened. After five
unwanted touches the whole town hears, and other unicorns run from you. Apologise to a unicorn
you wronged ("sorry", "forgive me", …) to take one strike off (once per 2 minutes per pony).
Strikes are kept in your world save.

**Princesses:** the fifth unwanted touch of her own horn is the last. Luna banishes you to the
moon: about a minute alone under the stars, then a whole day has passed and you wake at home.
Celestia and Twilight teleport you to a random spot in the Everfree Forest. Everypony hears of it.

Never affects fillies or colts, or a young player.

## Install

Put `horn-touch-v1.0.0.pck` in the game's `mods` folder and switch it on in **Settings → Mods**.

## Build from source

Copy this folder into `res://mods/horn_touch/` in the game project, then run `mods/build_packs.sh horn_touch`.

Testing from source: `-- --mod-src horn_touch` (add `--horn-touch-debug` to print what it does).
