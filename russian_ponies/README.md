# Russian Ponies

- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Made for:** Home: Equestria 0.3.0-maredev
- **Works on:** desktop and VR (nothing to press)

Ponies understand Russian and always answer in Russian. Type to them in Russian and they talk back in it.

## What it changes

- **Conversations:** every pony replies in natural, spoken Russian, in her own voice and manner. Terms of address and catchphrases get Russian equivalents, and pony and place names follow the Russian dub (Твайлайт Спаркл, Понивиль).
- **Group chats and overheard chatter** between ponies are in Russian too.
- Expression tags, actions and the game's internal notes stay as the game expects, so ponies still smile, walk, follow and so on.
- Nothing is written to your prompt files. Your own prompt edits in **Settings → Prompts** still apply.

## Install

Put `russian-ponies-v1.0.0.pck` in the game's `mods` folder and switch it on in **Settings → Mods**. To go back to English, switch it off and reload your world.

## Things to know

- **Type, don't speak:** the game's speech recognition only understands English, and pony voices only speak English. Use typed chat with the pony voices off ("ponese" works fine).
- **Menus, prompts and tooltips stay in English.**
- **Who you're talking to:** saying a pony's name in Russian doesn't pick her out of a group, because the game only reads names in Latin letters. Look at the pony you're talking to, or write her name in English letters ("Twilight, ...").
- **Your AI model matters:** most large models speak good Russian. Smaller or cheaper ones may drift back into English now and then, because the ponies' memories and background are in English.
- A few fixed lines that don't come from the AI (the courier, for example) stay in English. Letters are not changed: a pony usually answers in the language you write in.
- **Princess Teleport:** asking for a teleport only works in English, so in Russian she walks you there instead. Danger teleports still happen.
- Not yet tested with a real AI model.
