# Grooming

A Home: Equestria mod that lets you groom ponies: brush their mane and tail, brush their coat, and preen a pegasus's or alicorn's wings. Each action has its own animation, and finishing a grooming brings you and the pony closer.

- **Version:** 1.0.3
- **Made for:** Home: Equestria 0.3.0-maredev
- **Works on:** desktop and VR

## Install

1. Put `grooming-v1.0.3.pck` in your mods folder. In game, **Settings → Mods → Open mods folder** opens it.

   | System | Mods folder |
   | --- | --- |
   | Windows | `%APPDATA%\home-equestria-public\mods\` |
   | Linux | `~/.local/share/home-equestria-public/mods/` |
   | Quest | `Android/data/org.homeequestria.game/files/mods/` |

2. Restart the game.

The first time you load a world with the mod on, a **Mane & Tail Brush** and a **Coat Brush** appear in your briefcase. If the briefcase is full, they arrive the next time you load the world with room in it.

## How to groom

### Mane & tail

Hold the Mane & Tail Brush and stroke her mane or tail.

- **Desktop:** look at her mane or tail and hold the mouse button.
- **VR:** brush her with it.

While you brush her mane, she tilts her head into the brush and nods softly with each stroke. While you brush her tail, she lifts it aside, swishes it, and looks back over her shoulder at you.

### Coat

Hold the Coat Brush and stroke her neck, back, sides, shoulders, chest, rump, flanks, belly or legs.

- **Desktop:** look at her coat and hold the mouse button.
- **VR:** brush her with it.

She leans into the brush, lowers her head and sways with the strokes. A long brushing ends with a happy stretch.

### Wing preening (pegasi and alicorns)

1. **Open her wings.** Pet her wing with an empty hand. She spreads her wings with a flap and turns her head back toward them.
2. **Preen.** A few ruffled feathers now stick out of her wings.
   - **Desktop:** with nothing in your hand, aim at a ruffled feather and hold the mouse button.
   - **VR:** put your fingers or palm on a ruffled feather.

   Each feather wiggles, comes loose and drifts to the ground.
3. **Finish.** Once every ruffled feather is done, she shakes her wings out with three big flaps and folds them.

Her wings stay open for 10 seconds between preens. If you step away for longer, she folds them again.

On desktop, a hint under the crosshair shows what a click will do, along with your progress, such as `Hold click · Brush coat 40%` or `Hold click · Preen feather 2/4`.

On desktop, the brush resizes to suit the pony while it's on her: smaller on fillies and colts, bigger on large ponies (0.8× to 1.6×). It goes back to normal size in your hand.

## What grooming does

When you finish a grooming, the pony:

- **sighs contentedly,**
- **gains friendship with you,**
- **gains love with you,** if she's an adult who is already fond of you or dating you,
- **trusts you a little more,**
- **remembers it,** so her AI can bring it up in conversation.

A grooming is finished after about 10 seconds of mane strokes, 8 of tail strokes or 14 of coat strokes, or once every ruffled feather is preened.

Each pony gives this bonus at most once every 10 minutes per action. You can keep grooming as long as you like; it just won't add more until then.

## Things to know

- **Fillies and colts:** grooming is friendship only, with no love gain. The same applies if you're playing as a young pony.
- **Unwilling ponies:** a pony who is angry with you, or has asked for space, turns away and won't be groomed.
- **Sleeping ponies** can't be groomed.
- **Spa visitors** are left to the spa's own care.
- **Princess Celestia and Princess Luna** work too: their mane, tail and wings are built differently from other ponies', so the mod finds them its own way.
- **Switching it off:** you can turn the mod off any time in **Settings → Mods**. Any pony being groomed goes back to normal straight away.
- **Removing it:** the brushes are mod items. If you remove the mod, they stay in your briefcase as unknown items. Drop them first if you plan to remove it.

## Building from source

You need the game's source project (with its `source-data` zips unzipped into it) and Godot 4.5 stable.

1. Put this folder at `res://mods/grooming/`.
2. Open the project once so the sounds import:
   ```
   godot --headless --xr-mode off --path <project> --editor --quit
   ```
3. Build the pack:
   ```
   godot --headless --xr-mode off --path <project> --script res://tools/modding/build_mod.gd -- grooming
   ```

To test without building, run the game with the mod unpacked:
```
godot --xr-mode off --path <project> -- --mod-src grooming
```

The brush models in `models/` are generated by `tools/make_models.gd` from the Blender exports in `renders/grooming_brushes_v1.0.3/` (source: `renders/grooming_brushes_v1.0.3.blend`). `items/brush_item.gd` holds the older primitive brushes. Run the script again if you change the brushes:
```
godot --headless --xr-mode off --path <project> --script res://mods/grooming/tools/make_models.gd
```

### How it hooks into the game

- **Entry script:** `main.gd`. It hands out the brushes, detects grooming, runs the reactions and pays out the bonuses.
- **Item data:** `merge/world/food/definitions.json`. The brushes are added as non-edible items through the game's mergeable item data. Script extensions don't work on the released game's compiled scripts in 0.1.1.
- **Pony animation:** `groom_pose.gd`, a skeleton modifier. It only adds small bone rotations, well inside the rig's joint limits.
- **Desktop clicks:** `input_catcher.gd`, a small node placed last in the scene tree. It sees a brush or preening click before the briefcase does. Every other click passes through untouched.
