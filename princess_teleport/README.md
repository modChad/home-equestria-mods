# Princess Teleport

- **Version:** 1.1.0
- **Made for:** Home: Equestria 0.3.0-maredev
- **Works on:** desktop and VR

Princess Celestia, Princess Luna and Twilight Sparkle can teleport, and take you with them.

## How to use it

Stand next to one of them and ask her to teleport you somewhere far, e.g. "Can you teleport us back to Ponyville?" or "Zap us to Canterlot with your magic." Her horn lights up, you both vanish in a flash of her magic, and you reappear there together.

- **Only when you agree.** You have to ask to be teleported, or say yes when she offers ("Shall I teleport us?"). A plain "Take me to Canterlot" is walked, and "no", "don't teleport me" or "let's walk" always means walking.
- **Except in danger.** With a timberwolf close by, or in the Everfree after dark, she may teleport you both to safety without asking.

- **Far trips only.** Places more than about 150 m away are teleported to. Nearby places are walked to as normal.
- **Stay close.** You need to be within a few metres of her when she agrees. If you're further away, she walks there instead.
- **Where she can go.** She can teleport anywhere she could walk you to: the world's regions (Ponyville, Canterlot, Sweet Apple Acres…), landmarks and houses.

## Install

Put `princess-teleport-v1.1.0.pck` in the game's `mods` folder and switch it on in **Settings → Mods**.

## Things to know

- Far-away places can take a few seconds to load in when you arrive. She appears beside you once the ground there is ready.
- Your agreement counts for about 90 seconds after you say it.
- The log notes each teleport, and each far trip walked because you didn't ask (lines starting `[princess_teleport]`).
