# Princess Wings

- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Made for:** Home: Equestria 0.3.0-maredev
- **Works on:** desktop and VR (nothing to press)

Celestia's and Luna's open wings now grow from their shoulder blades, just behind the withers, instead of floating on top of the middle of the back.

## What it changes

- **Celestia:** open wings 10 cm further forward and 5 cm lower, so the roots sit over the tops of her forelegs and sink into her back.
- **Luna:** open wings 4.8 cm forward, 1.8 cm lower and 1.1 cm further out to each side.
- Both are placed by the same rule, taken from the ordinary pegasus body (Rainbow Dash): the wing root sits just behind the top of the foreleg, sunk into the back by 8.4% of the barrel's height, two thirds of the way from the spine to the side.

Only the open wings move. Their bodies, folded wings and skeletons are untouched, and everything that poses the wings (flying, excitement, the spa's half-open hold, preening) still drives them the same way. The shift grows and shrinks with the pony's size setting.

## Install

Put `princess_wings.pck` in the game's `mods` folder and switch it on in **Settings → Mods**. It switches on and off straight away; switched off, the wings go back to the game's placement.

## Things to know

- Other ponies' wings are not changed.
- Half open, the front edge of the wing reaches a little further forward than before, over the shoulder.
- The folded wings were already fitted to the bodies and are unchanged; the unfold from folded to open starts from the new spot.
