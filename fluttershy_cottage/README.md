# Fluttershy's Cottage

**1.4.2:** the old doorway on the sitting room's right wall, filled in since 1.2.0, is now flush:
the patch was never joined to the wall round it, which showed as dark lines inside and outside. That
stretch of wall is one piece now, and the skirting board runs unbroken across it (it had a gap at
the foot of the wall).

**1.4.1:** fixes from the first play-through of 1.4.0: the rugs no longer show a black square
round them, and the sitting room's right wall (between the curtained window and the back corner) is
plain wall again instead of a stretched plank patch with a saw-tooth edge.

**1.4.0:** the outside reworked after the show. One tall, rounded leafy mound drapes low over both
storeys, swelling into leaf clumps with a fringe of leaves along its hem. A rounded plaster
**porch** with a mossy cap and a birdhouse stands round the stable door, and one fat tan
**chimney** with the birdhouses rises from the sitting room's stove (the loft chimney is gone).
Windows have thick surrounds and brown lattices, with flower boxes on the ground floor; the loft's
round windows sit in plaster hoods with a plain cross. The loft's back window is a **casement**:
two leaves you open outward by hand. A new **kitchen window** above the sink, with yellow curtains.
In the loft: Fluttershy's own teal bed with the checked quilt (the working bed underneath is
unchanged), a round rug, a small table and pitcher, a **lantern** on the desk that lights at night
and switches by hand, hanging birdhouses, nests and vines on the beams, and a baluster railing
round the stairs.

**1.3.0:** a show-style outside: one leafy grass mound over both storeys with a scalloped fringe,
ivy down the back and sides, open window frames with wooden crosses, and two chimneys, one
rising from the sitting room's stove (with the birdhouses) and one from the loft fireplace. A new
red-brown arched **stable door** at the front: both halves swing out, the top half opens on its
own so you can lean on the ledge, and opening the bottom opens both. A storeroom under the loft
(barrels, crates, a pantry, feed sacks, a lantern and its own back door), reached from the
kitchen through a pony-height arched doorway where the kitchen window used to be. The loft
fireplace is its own model now, ready for a fire later.

**1.2.0:** a proper outside, built for the new room from the game cottage's own materials:
grass-thatch roofs over both storeys, plaster walls with wood posts and beams, a door frame, a
flagstone base and chimney, the closed space under the loft's overhang, bushes, window-box
greenery and a birdhouse. Inside, Discopears' own prop layout: the fireplace, round table, sofa
and armchair (with seats), rugs, kitchen table and benches (with seats), pans, garlic, book rows,
and the loft's chest and desk. Fluttershy's bed stands where the set has hers, the working stove
on the kitchen's front wall, the readable bookshelf in the loft, and standing lamps light the
sitting room, kitchen and loft at night. Ponies walk all of it, up to the loft. 1.1.0 left the
cottage with no way in; 1.2.0 changes no game script.

- Scale 0.345 of the set's .blend (the size the game already uses for this set).
- Textures are the game's own copies of the set's and the old exterior's textures; the pack carries
  only meshes.
- Switching the mod off in Settings → Mods puts the game's cottage back at once.
